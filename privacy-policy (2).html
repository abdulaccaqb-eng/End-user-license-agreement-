Your privacy matters. Book Swift Pros LLC is committed to protecting the financial data you entrust to us. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.
01

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Book Swift Pros LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), registered in Sheridan, Wyoming, USA, collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our bookkeeping and accounting services, including our integration with Intuit QuickBooks Online via the Intuit API.

This policy applies to all clients, visitors, and users who interact with our services, whether through direct engagement, our website, or third-party platforms such as Fiverr or Upwork.

By using our services or authorizing our QuickBooks integration, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Category Examples Purpose
Identity Data Name, business name, email address Client account setup and communication
Financial Data Bank transactions, invoices, expenses, P&L, balance sheets Bookkeeping and reconciliation services
QuickBooks Data Accounts, transactions, vendor/customer records (via API) Delivering bookkeeping services via QBO integration
Communication Data Emails, messages, instructions you send us Service coordination and support
Technical Data IP address, browser type (website visitors only) Website security and analytics

We collect only the minimum data required to perform the services you have engaged us for.

03

QuickBooks Online Data Access

Our integration with Intuit QuickBooks Online uses OAuth 2.0 authorization. This means:

  • We never store your QuickBooks username or password.
  • You authorize our access directly through Intuit's secure authorization screens.
  • We receive a secure access token that allows us to read and write data within your QuickBooks account on your behalf.
  • The specific scopes of data we access are limited to what is needed for your bookkeeping engagement (e.g., accounts, transactions, reports).
  • You can revoke our access at any time from your Intuit Account > Authorized Apps settings.
Scope of Access: We access only the QuickBooks data categories required for your specific engagement — such as chart of accounts, transactions, customers, vendors, and financial reports. We do not access payroll data, employee records, or tax filings unless explicitly agreed upon in your service contract.

This application uses Intuit's APIs but is not endorsed or certified by Intuit Inc. Use of QuickBooks is subject to Intuit's own Privacy Statement.

04

How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Service Delivery: Categorizing transactions, reconciling accounts, preparing financial statements, and generating reports.
  • Communication: Responding to your queries, sending deliverables, and providing service updates.
  • Quality Assurance: Reviewing work internally to ensure accuracy and completeness.
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting applicable legal, tax, or regulatory obligations.
  • Business Operations: Invoicing, record-keeping, and dispute resolution.

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, advertising, or any purpose outside of delivering your bookkeeping services.

05

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We may share limited data with:

  • Our Team Members: Authorized bookkeeping staff who work on your account, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Service Platforms: Intuit (QuickBooks), Google Workspace, and similar tools used to deliver our services — only to the extent necessary.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, court order, or regulatory authority.

All third-party tools we use are subject to their own privacy policies. We select partners who maintain strong data protection standards.

06

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted storage and transmission of financial documents.
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only.
  • Secure handling of API tokens and OAuth credentials.
  • Regular review of access permissions and internal security practices.

While we strive to use commercially reasonable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

In the event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you promptly in accordance with applicable laws.

07

Data Retention

We retain your financial data for as long as:

  • You remain an active client of Book Swift Pros LLC, or
  • Required by applicable law (e.g., tax record retention requirements), typically 7 years for accounting records.

Upon request or upon termination of the engagement, we will securely delete or return your data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

QuickBooks API access tokens are revoked immediately upon termination of services.

08

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Revocation: Revoke QuickBooks API authorization at any time through your Intuit account settings.
  • Portability: Request a portable copy of your financial data in a standard format.
  • Objection: Object to certain uses of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@bookswiftpros.com. We will respond within 30 days.

09

Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use minimal cookies for functionality and analytics purposes. We do not use cookies to track your behavior for advertising. Types of cookies we may use:

  • Essential cookies: Necessary for the website to function correctly.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site (e.g., Google Analytics, anonymized data only).

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.
